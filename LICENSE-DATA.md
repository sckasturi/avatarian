## What this covers

- **The glyph drawings** — `designs/`, the generated SVGs in
  `site/assets/glyphs/`, and the same shapes where they are embedded
  (`site/js/manifest.js`, `wiki/Avatarian-css-only.css`, and the path data
  in `tools/build_glyphs.py`).
- **The corpus** — `corpus/attested.json`, `corpus/conventions.json`,
  `corpus/uncatalogued/`, and the generated `site/js/corpus.js`: the
  transcriptions, glosses and notes.

## What it does not cover

- **The Avatarian script itself**, and the decipherment it is drawn from.
  Neither is this project's work; see `AVATARIAN.md` § Credit for whose it is.
- **The source images** in `site/sources/` (and `corpus/sources/`). They are
  screenshots, photographs and posts kept as provenance for the corpus. They
  belong to their respective owners and are **not** licensed by this
  repository.
- **`reference/avatarian_key.svg`** and the tracings made from it in
  `site/assets/reference/` — the key chart is credited in `AVATARIAN.md`.
- **The pronunciation dictionary** (`site/js/lexicon.js`), which is derived
  from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary — see `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
