Every word someone has actually seen written in Avatarian, drawn from its recorded spelling. These are observations, not guesses. The translator derives anything it hasn't seen, but everything here was read off a real source.

Structural search — find a sound, a block, or a run of sounds

Grammar: [[top:|bottom:]sound | top bottom] [@cc @cv @vc @vv @null] or /sounds/. Sounds read like the translator — sh, ng, uh, or raw IPA. So g @cc finds /g/ paired with another consonant, and never /ŋ/. The sound is optional: a shape on its own — @cc, or picking a block shape with the sound left on any — finds every block of that shape. Two sounds find one exact block, top then bottom: m z is /m/ over /z/, and s - is /s/ over a null. Sounds between slashes search the pronunciation instead: /e ɪ/ finds that run of sounds however the blocks split it, nulls ignored. Between slashes IPA wins, so e i u j are the IPA sounds; other codes still work (/ey ɪ/). Matching blocks are ringed in each word.

Underlined means attested, a word the corpus has seen. Hover a word (or tab to it) to see the source it was read off. Cards outlined in colour are contested: the sources disagree on the spelling, and the most-attested one is shown. A faded ✳ in a word is a character too unclear to read in the source, a gap in the reading rather than the spelling.
