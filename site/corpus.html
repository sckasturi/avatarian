Every word someone has actually seen written in Avatarian, drawn from its recorded spelling. These are observations, not guesses. The translator derives anything it hasn't seen, but everything here was read off a real source.

Structural search — find a sound by the block it sits in

Grammar: [top:|bottom:]sound [@cc @cv @vc @vv @null]. Sounds read like the translator — sh, ng, uh, or raw IPA. So g @cc finds /g/ paired with another consonant, and never /ŋ/. Matching blocks are ringed in each word.

Underlined means attested, a word the corpus has seen. Hover a word (or tab to it) to see the source it was read off. Cards outlined in colour are contested: the sources disagree on the spelling, and the most-attested one is shown. A faded ✳ in a word is a character too unclear to read in the source, a gap in the reading rather than the spelling.
