Seen a word written in Avatarian — on a poster, a letter, a stamp, a frame of the film? Add it to the corpus. Read the glyphs off the image yourself (the tool previews and suggests, but never reads the picture for you), attach the image as proof, and submit. Your submission opens a pull request that a maintainer reviews before anything enters the corpus — so take your best reading; it doesn't have to be perfect.
