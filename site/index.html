Avatarian Translator

Type English, or type the sounds directly. Everything is editable, so you can correct anything the converter gets wrong.

Type ARPAbet codes — plain QWERTY, no special characters: K AH T AA R AH. IPA works too if you prefer. Separate sounds with spaces and words with /. The drawing updates as you type.
Anything in (parentheses) captions that word instead of being read as a sound — M EH T AX L 0 B EH N D IH NG (metalbending).
0 is the empty-slot filler (the ∪ cup for vowel slots). Add $ or % after a sound to force its top or bottom form, e.g. S$ — needed where the rule isn't known yet.

dashed box = no confirmed glyph for that sound yet
